Even though they generate billions of rupees through UPI QR codes, they are locked out of bank loans because they have zero credit bureau (CIBIL) history and zero collateral.

## 2. Technical Architecture & Deep Dives

### Q: How does your Alternative Credit Scoring work without a CIBIL score?
**Answer:**
- Traditional credit scores rely on past loan repayment history and collateral. Micro-merchants have neither.
- Instead, MicroSetu looks at **cash-flow velocity and consistency** using 13 engineered features:
  1. **Operational Discipline**: Active trading days per month (out of 30).
  2. **Transaction Velocity & Stability**: Daily count and coefficient of variation of daily revenue ($CV = \sigma / \mu$).
  3. **Customer Retention**: Ratio of repeat UPI handles transacting $>3$ times/month.
  4. **Digital Hygiene**: Soundbox adoption (reduces dispute fraud) and failed transaction dispute rates.
- These features feed into a **Gradient Boosting Classifier** that estimates the default probability, which is scaled non-linearly to a **SetuScore (300 to 900)** with Explainable AI feature attribution (SHAP-style) so lenders see the exact reasons behind loan approval.

### Q: How did you design the Anti-Fraud & Spoof Detection Shield?
**Answer:**
- Street vendors frequently lose money to customers showing spoofed payment screenshots or fake modded APKs (e.g. fake Paytm apps that play audio without moving actual money).
- MicroSetu addresses this with a 4-tier validation pipeline:
  1. **NPCI UTR Checksum & Switch Routing**: Validates the 12-digit reference against Indian banking year switch rules.
  2. **In-Memory Sliding Window Cache**: Prevents duplicate UTR replay attacks within 24 hours.
  3. **Timestamp Freshness Heuristics**: Detects future-dated or stale timestamps generated by screenshot generators.
  4. **Image & OCR Signature Analysis**: Checks for font metric deviations and localized compression artifacts around amount strings.

### Q: Why did you choose FastAPI over Flask or Django?
**Answer:**
- **Asynchronous Concurrency**: FastAPI is built on Starlette and ASGI, which delivers high throughput for concurrent transaction streams and real-time simulations.
- **Pydantic Validation**: Automatic schema validation, data serialization, and interactive OpenAPI documentation out-of-the-box.
- **Low Latency**: Inference execution time is under 15ms.

### Q: How does the Vernacular Voice-First Ledger work?
**Answer:**
- Micro-vendors are often semi-literate and cannot type into complex spreadsheets.
- Using the **Web Speech API**, the merchant simply speaks: *"Aaj 450 rupaye ki sabzi kharidi"*.
- The natural language regex and semantic parser extracts the amount (`₹450`), transaction type (`EXPENSE`), and category (`Vegetables & Produce`) to update the merchant's real-time P&L statement.
