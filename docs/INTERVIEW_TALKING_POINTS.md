# Interview Guide & Technical Talking Points: MicroSetu

Use this cheat sheet to ace technical, system design, machine learning, and product questions about the project during interviews.

---

## 1. The 45-Second Elevator Pitch

> *"Over 90% of India's workforce is informal—street vendors, chai stall owners, and small shopkeepers. While UPI gave millions of them a high-velocity digital cash flow, they remain locked out of formal micro-loans because traditional banks require CIBIL scores, salary slips, and collateral.*
> 
> *To solve this, I built **MicroSetu**, an applied FinTech platform that turns high-frequency UPI transaction streams into an alternative credit score (SetuScore, 300–900). It features an explainable underwriting engine, a voice-first Hindi expense ledger for semi-literate merchants, an anti-fraud shield against fake screenshot scams, and automated PM SVANidhi micro-loan tranche recommendations."*

## 2. Technical Deep Dives & System Design Q&A

### Q: Why do you need an Alternative Credit Score if CIBIL already exists?
**Key Points to Answer:**
* **The Structural Gap:** CIBIL is backward-looking and requires existing debt history or formal payroll records. Informal micro-merchants have neither, creating a catch-22 (cannot get credit without credit history).
* **Cash-Flow as the Proxy:** A street vendor transacting ₹1,500/day across 28 active days per month demonstrates strong operational discipline and debt repayment capacity.
* **13 Engineered Signals:**
  1. *Operational Discipline:* Active trading days per month (consistency indicator).
  2. *Cash Flow Stability:* Daily turnover coefficient of variation ($CV = \sigma / \mu$) to identify irregular or seasonal volatility.
  3. *Customer Loyalty:* Percentage of repeat payer handles transacting $>3$ times per month.
  4. *Digital Soundbox & Dispute Hygiene:* Low dispute rate indicates legitimate trade rather than fraudulent claims.

---

### Q: How did you train and calibrate the underwriting model?
**Key Points to Answer:**
* **Synthetic Cohort Calibration:** Since private banking UPI streams are protected under data privacy laws, I calibrated a cohort of 1,200+ merchants across 6 informal retail categories (tea stalls, vegetable carts, kirana, street food, auto drivers, flower sellers) based on published empirical distributions:
  * 86% small-ticket transactions $\le$ ₹500 (matching NPCI retail statistics).
  * Income distributions aligned with field studies (*Mallick & Singla 2025, Devi 2025*).
* **Algorithm Choice:** Used **Gradient Boosting** over opaque deep learning because credit underwriting requires strict model interpretability, auditability, and regulatory compliance (RBI Fair Lending Practices).
* **Addressing High Model Accuracy / AUC on Prototypes:**
  * *"On a synthetic prototype dataset where risk rules are defined mathematically, the classifier achieves high separation. In a real-world production environment with noisy bank logs and external macroeconomic shocks, I'd expect the ROC-AUC to normalize around 0.75–0.82. The primary value here is the end-to-end feature pipeline, real-time scoring, and explainable credit drivers."*

---

### Q: How does the Anti-Fraud Shield work against fake payment scams?
**Key Points to Answer:**
* **The Street Problem:** Customers often use fake payment apps (modded APKs) or screenshot generators to show fake green-tick confirmation screens.
* **Multi-Layer Defense:**
  1. *NPCI Switch Code Validation:* Checks the 12-digit numeric structure against Indian banking switch conventions.
  2. *In-Memory Sliding Window Cache:* Stores processed UTRs with timestamps to catch and block replay attacks within 24 hours.
  3. *Timestamp Freshness Heuristics:* Detects future-dated timestamps (generated by modded apps) or stale receipts (>2 hours old).
  4. *Audio Verification Decoupling:* Voice soundbox announcements are only triggered after the backend verifies the transaction claim.

---

### Q: How does the Vernacular Voice-First Ledger work without expensive LLMs?
**Key Points to Answer:**
* **Edge-First Architecture:** Utilizes the browser's native **Web Speech API** (`webkitSpeechRecognition` / `SpeechSynthesis`) for zero-latency speech-to-text without recurring third-party API costs.
* **Deterministic NLP Slot Extraction:** Uses specialized regex and semantic tokenizers to parse natural Hindi/Hinglish phrasing (*e.g., "Aaj 450 rupaye ki sabzi kharidi mandi se"*):
  * Identifies transaction intent (`EXPENSE` vs `INCOME`).
  * Extracts numerical values and currency tokens (`₹450`).
  * Classifies expense into operational categories (*Produce, Inventory, Wages, Logistics*).
* **Outcome:** Provides semi-literate vendors with automated, zero-typing daily P&L tracking.

---

### Q: Why did you choose FastAPI and Vanilla JavaScript?
**Key Points to Answer:**
* **Backend:** FastAPI's asynchronous ASGI architecture handles concurrent transaction streams with sub-20ms inference latency, built-in Pydantic validation, and automatic OpenAPI schema generation.
* **Frontend:** Vanilla JS with CSS design tokens provided instant load times, zero build-step overhead, and direct programmatic control over native browser APIs (Canvas BharatQR generation and Web Speech API).
